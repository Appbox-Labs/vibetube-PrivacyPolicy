# VibeTube Privacy Policy

## Privacy Policy for VibeTube Mobile App

**Effective Date:** September 24, 2026  
**Contact:** appboxlabscontact@gmail.com

### Introduction
VibeTube ("we", "our", or "the App") is an independent media player and manager that allows users to discover and play royalty-licensed online audio/video content, save open-license media for offline use, and play local media files stored on their device. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is handled, and your choices.

By installing and using VibeTube, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

### Summary
- **No Account Required:** We do not require account creation, registration, or personal information (such as name, email, or phone number) to use the App.
- **On-Device Storage:** All user data, including playback preferences, favorites, playlists, search history, and offline saves, are stored locally on your device.
- **Internet Usage:** Internet access is used solely to fetch open-license media streams, retrieve metadata, and display advertisements.
- **No Central User Database:** We do not collect or store personal profiles on external servers.

### Information We Access or Process

#### 3.1 Information Stored on Your Device
The App stores the following data locally on your device:
- App settings and user preferences (e.g., playback speed, equalizer settings)
- Search history, favorites, and custom playlists
- Download metadata and saved media files chosen by the user
- Temporary cached media for uninterrupted playback

This data remains strictly on your device until you clear app data or uninstall VibeTube.

#### 3.2 Local Media Access
With your explicit permission, VibeTube accesses local audio and video files on your device to display your personal media library and artwork. Local media is used strictly for offline playback within the App and is never uploaded to any external server.

#### 3.3 Online Content Requests
When searching, streaming, or downloading content, the App sends standard HTTP network requests to open-access media APIs and public domain repositories (such as CCmixter.org and Archive.org). These requests include standard technical data:
- Search terms entered by the user
- Technical metadata required for media streaming
- Device/network parameters typical of standard HTTP connections (e.g., IP address, user-agent)

#### 3.4 Notifications and Background Services
With user consent, VibeTube utilizes background services and system notifications for:
- Active download progress and completion alerts
- Background playback controls ("Now Playing" notification widget)
- Resuming unfinished downloads after device reboots

#### 3.5 Advertising & Third-Party SDKs
VibeTube integrates third-party advertising networks to monetize the application. These ad networks may collect anonymous technical identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID, IP address, and device model) to serve contextual ads:
- **Meta Audience Network:** Used for serving advertisements. Review Meta's Privacy Policy at https://www.facebook.com/about/privacy
- **Google AdMob / Play Services:** Used for ad delivery and infrastructure stability. Review Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy

#### 3.6 Information We Do NOT Collect
VibeTube does NOT collect, request, or store:
- Personal identifiers (Name, Email, Phone Number, Home Address)
- Precise GPS Location Data
- Contacts, SMS, or Call Logs
- Financial, Banking, or Payment Data

### How We Use Information
On-device storage and technical parameters are processed solely to:
- Provide smooth playback, search functionality, offline caching, and media management
- Maintain user preferences and custom playlists
- Render advertisements through authorized monetization partners
- Maintain stability, prevent crashes, and optimize network requests

### Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure
We do not sell, trade, or transfer your data to outside parties. Data is processed externally only as required for core service operation:
- Public Domain & Open-License Content Repositories accessed by network requests
- Authorized Advertising Networks (Meta Audience Network & Google AdMob)

### Rights, Data Retention, and Deletion
Since all user data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over your data. You can delete all processed data at any time by:
- Clearing the cache and search history via App Settings
- Clearing storage/data for VibeTube under Android System Settings
- Uninstalling the application (this permanently removes all app-private storage)

### Android Permissions Requested

| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE | Stream online media, manage network requests, and load ads |
| READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_AUDIO | Access local audio files to build your local offline library |
| WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE | Save royalty-free media files directly to device storage |
| POST_NOTIFICATIONS | Display playback status and download progress notifications |
| FOREGROUND_SERVICE | Maintain uninterrupted audio playback and active downloads in the background |
| RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED | Safely resume incomplete user downloads after system reboots |

### Content Licensing & Legal Safeguard
All media tracks accessible via online search are provided under open licenses, primarily Creative Commons (CC BY / CC BY-SA 3.0), sourced from public domain databases including CCmixter.org and Archive.org.

VibeTube is an independent media player. It does not host, stream, or distribute copyrighted commercial music from proprietary streaming platforms.

### Children's Privacy
VibeTube is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has accessed the app and provided data, please contact us for immediate resolution.

### Security
We utilize standard Android Application Sandboxing to secure on-device data. However, users are responsible for maintaining physical security and access controls on their personal devices.
